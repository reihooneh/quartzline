How the planner got there

    Quartzline is open source (MIT). It runs entirely in your browser: no sign-in, no tracking, and setups you load never leave your device. Its clay and kiln figures are typical values for comparing schedules, not measurements of your clay or your kiln: test a new schedule on pieces you can spare, and follow your kiln maker's safety instructions.
